01 / ABOUT

Rooted in Chitral.
Curious about the world.

My journey is shaped by curiosity, community, technology, and the belief that young people can contribute today.

I started programming at age 11, learning through experimentation and building projects. I later completed Harvard University's CS50 Introduction to Computer Science, strengthening my foundations in computer science and problem-solving.

Growing up in Chitral has made me aware of the opportunities and challenges faced by students in remote communities. I want to use technology and collaboration to make useful opportunities easier to discover and local problems easier to address.

Beyond coding, I enjoy debate, cultural exchange, environmental work, learning Japanese, and collaborating with students from different backgrounds. I believe leadership begins with listening, taking responsibility, and turning an idea into a practical first step.
